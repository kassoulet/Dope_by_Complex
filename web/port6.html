Part 6: a palette that breathes and lights that follow the camera

Block 6 of DOPE.EXE: the engine of part 5 on a city. part6.js. Same two fillers, the same wobbling row table (here cs:0x10, rebuilt by the timer from a brightness [0x176e] that the script moves), shld 10; the engine differences are addresses, a face list at 0x3200 and a sort starting one bit lower (12 passes instead of 11), and neg edx in the vertex stage, which mirrors the lighting coordinates.

Lights that follow the view (0x2f05)

The 256 wide lookup strip the polygons are lit from is repainted every frame in its right half: every pixel is faded by 0x10 ((ax - 0x1010) & 0x3f3f, two pixels at once) and the pattern pixel is added: a 64 x 64 pattern, each pixel doubled both ways, whose segment is chosen by the camera's angle ((-angle & 0x3fff) >> 9, 32 patterns). So lights fade out behind a turning camera. The strip is an array of the image shared with the objects' lookup, as in the original: the port's image is the 1 MB memory.

A palette cross fade every tick

The timer interrupt, after the part's hook, uploads colours 16..207 (0x3995): clamp(rampA[paletteA[i]] + rampB[paletteB[i]]) where paletteA is loaded at the start from the data and paletteB is a second 192 colour palette in the block, and the ramps (ramp() of part 4) are built by the frame from the fade position [0x3259], which the script moves. Day to night, with no pixel touched.

The end

The script's 16 steps take 3,990 ticks; then the DAC is read back and faded to black in 37 steps (bx = 256, 249, ..., colour = colour * bx >> 8), one per retrace.
